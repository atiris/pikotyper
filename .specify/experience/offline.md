# Offline

## What
- Installable PWA. After the first visit, the whole game (including the language files of
  the chosen language) works offline. A new version is picked up on the next start; the version changes whenever any
  file changes, pictures and music included. Music is not installed up front: the tracks
  of the worlds played are kept as they play.

## Why
- School Wi-Fi is unreliable; offline play also shows that no data leaves the device.
