# Help

## What
- A help page for the child, in short, big, friendly text (sk and en):
  - **How to type**: a few tips on sitting, the home keys F and J, thumbs over the
    space bar, each finger with its own keys, eyes on the screen, accuracy before speed,
    short breaks.
  - **Buttons on the map** and **Buttons in a level**: every top button drawn exactly as
    it looks in the game, each with one line on what it does.
  - **How to play**: type the word on the object, nothing bad happens when it gets away,
    stars come from accuracy, new letters come with good typing, Esc pauses.
- A round "?" button in the map's top buttons opens it (with its own address); a "Help"
  button in a level's pause card opens it over the paused level.
- A purple round "?" button in a level's top bar pauses the level and opens a card: what to do
  now (the level's intro and one tip), how to do best (accuracy first, then a steady rhythm,
  the paw when stuck, repeat for three stars, rest after a few levels) and the real buttons.
  Escape or "Continue" closes it and resumes.
- The round close button in the top right corner (the one of every dialog) or Escape returns where the child came from: the map, or the paused level.
- Works on a 1024x600 screen; the page scrolls, the close button stays in sight.
- Explains the paw (what it shows, when to press), the ring with the level's number beside the pause button (levels done, this one, to come, a web for the big challenge), the rescue mission and the badge, each in one short sentence. The level help card has the same text, left-aligned and without the super star.

## Why
- Children do not guess what symbols mean, and the parent is not always next to them.
- Opening help from a level must not end the run, so the level only waits paused.
