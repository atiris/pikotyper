# Lesson progression

## What
- Keys unlock gradually, starting from the home row (`f j`, then `d k`, `s l`, `a` and
  `ô`/`;`), then the upper row, the lower row, then (Slovak) the diacritics track,
  capitals with Shift, and digits.
- A new key is introduced alone first (where it is, which finger), then mixed with known keys.
- A key counts as mastered when its recent accuracy and rhythm reach a target; then the
  next key unlocks. The parent can override and pick the active keys by hand.
- Adaptive practice: keys with low accuracy or slow reaction appear more often in words.
- Story levels use the child's current key set, so the story and the lessons move together.
- Free practice mode: calm typing of words from the current key set, no story.

## Why
- Gradual unlocking with weak-key weighting (Keybr, Typing.com) is what users praise most
  for real progress ([research](../../.research/features/lesson-progression.md)).
