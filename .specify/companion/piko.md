# Piko

## What
- Piko is a cute companion (default name "Piko") shown on screen during play. The parent
  picks its animal kind when creating the profile (free choice) and can change name and
  kind in the settings. Kinds: fox (default), cat, bunny, owl, dog,
  bear, panda, penguin, lion. All kinds share one front-view body shape, so every
  accessory fits every kind.
- Piko encourages on mistakes (never blames), celebrates successes, gives hints and leads
  the breaks. Messages come from a localized pool plus the parent's own custom
  encouraging messages.
- Accessories unlock by progress (finishing levels and worlds, mastering keys), never by
  chance (each player earns their own; siblings share a computer): hats, glasses, scarves and bows, vests and shirts, wings, capes and backpacks,
  shoes, things to hold (wand, balloon, lantern). One item per body area (head, eyes,
  neck, body, back, hand, feet); the child mixes them freely in the "Buddy and wardrobe"
  screen.
- In the same screen the child renames the companion (up to 16 characters, never empty)
  and picks another animal kind. Other kinds are earned by stars: every 6 stars over all
  levels unlock the next one (cat, bunny, owl, dog, bear, panda, penguin, lion, then fox;
  the starting kind is skipped). Locked kinds show a lock and how many stars are missing;
  a new friend is announced on the level results. Accessories stay on when the kind
  changes.
  A back item can have a front part (a cape's clasp, a backpack's straps) that lies
  over the body while the item itself stays behind.
- After each story level that has a next one, Piko asks a curious question about the
  world's theme (real facts about animals, space, eggs) and gives the answer only after
  the next level is finished.
- **Paw help**: fifteen words in a row without a mistake charge one paw (three at most,
  kept between levels). In story levels and rescue missions, Enter (or the round paw
  button next to pause: a paw in the companion's color, ringed by three arcs for the
  charges, with an Enter badge) sends Piko's paws onto the keyboard in
  place of the hands, and Piko types the three closest words with his own rhythm. These
  keys never count in the child's statistics, accuracy or rhythm, and the child's own
  keys wait meanwhile. Backspace switches the paw to automatic (the button pulses gently
  and shows an `A` badge):
  Piko then helps by himself in a hard moment, when the screen holds three words (or as
  many as the level allows), the closest one has used most of its time and the child is
  not typing it.
- Clicking Piko opens the statistics overlay ([statistics](../profiles/statistics.md)).
- After world 5 a newly hatched friend (a baby dragon) joins; more friends come from
  rescue missions.

## Why
- A companion that gives gentle, personal feedback replaces punishment and makes the
  child's progress visible and their own.
- Naming the friend and earning new ones by playing makes the companion the child's own
  and gives one more gentle reason to collect stars.
- An open question is a gentle reason to play one more level, and the answer teaches
  something on the way.
- Paw help is a reward for accuracy that makes play more fun and takes the edge off a
  crowded moment, without blurring what the child really typed.
