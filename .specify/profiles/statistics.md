# Statistics

## What
- Per key: presses, accuracy, average reaction time, and the most frequent wrong key
  typed instead.
- An overlay opened by clicking Piko gives the child a friendly view: a keyboard colored
  by how well each key goes, the 3 keys to practice, and a button to practice them now.
- Trends over sessions (accuracy, rhythm), shown as simple charts in the parent area.

## Why
- Knowing the problem keys turns mistakes into a clear, small next step.
